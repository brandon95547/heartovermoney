ART + CHARITY: CHARLOTTE
Projected impact

GOAL
- Event cost: $10,000. The progress bar fills as tickets, food and drink, products and art sell.

WHERE THE MONEY GOES
- First, the event's cost.
- Then, half of what's raised goes to Charlotte Mission Partners for local outreach.
- The other half helps send the mission to the next city: travel, music, filming and the next
  limited-edition collection.

WHAT LASTS
- Every piece of art sold is numbered and never reprinted in its size or format.
- Each comes with a certificate of authenticity and a QR code to the Charlotte story, so the
  city stays part of the piece's history.

These figures are projections. Tonight's Scoreboard shows the real totals as sales happen.
