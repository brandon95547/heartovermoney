ART + CHARITY: CHARLOTTE
Event plan

Where: Uptown Charlotte
Host: Heart Over Money
Charity: Charlotte Mission Partners

THE EVENING
- Doors open: guests claim their collector cards at the door with the code in My Cards.
- Worship and music, with the musicians who recorded "Charlotte (Live)".
- The Charlotte collection on display. VIP members can buy limited-edition art in the app.
- Food and drink, ordered in the app and picked up at the bar.
- The live auction: the Founders Edition Canvas, the Collector Print Bundle and hand-painted
  Good News Shoes. Bidding opens in the app when the auction starts.

THE EVENT CONTRACT
- The event's cost is the goal shown on the progress bar.
- Every sale is counted on Tonight's Scoreboard as it happens.
- Half of everything raised after the cost goes to Charlotte Mission Partners. The rest sends
  the mission to the next city.

PHOTOS
- Guests post photos to the event's photo wall. Every photo on the wall can be downloaded.
- Photos that break the rules are removed by an admin, or by five thumbs-downs from guests.
